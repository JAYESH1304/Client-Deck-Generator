%s Our Understanding
Aideo helps providers automate medical coding by analysing physician notes in EHRs to find relevant evidence for submitting claims with specific codes (e.g., ICD, CPT, MIPS, LONIC)
Aideo gets paid by providers for successful coding of the codes
Currently Aideo products are well recognized and used by multiple clients for specific out-patient services / specialties  
Aideo has plans to expand their offerings to other outpatient disciplines and to inpatient services
Immediate Objectives
Help Aideo expand their product offerings to inpatient services
Help Aideo improve the coding accuracy for outpatient services such as Radiology
Help Aideo in architecting the solution / product into a robust and scalable solution and offering to Aideo’s clients

%s AI Autocoding Pod Charter:
objectives, guiding principles, deliverables and team.
To develop AI-based autocoding solutions for prioritized inpatient departments and enhance the autocoding accuracy of Radiology departments.
To automate medical coding process, ensuring accurate coding practices while reducing administrative burden on healthcare professionals
POD OBJECTIVE
Develop specialized models for coding prioritized inpatient department claims leveraging GenAI and NLP models to accurately determine appropriate ICD / CPT codes to document diagnosis and procedures
Develop specialized models for coding Radiology claims leveraging GenAI, NLP and Image Analytics models to accurately determine appropriate ICD / CPT / HCPCS codes to document diagnosis and procedures
Implement intelligent coding assistance features, such as code suggestions, code validation, and coding guidelines, to support and enhance the coding process for healthcare professionals.
KEY EPICS / FEATURES
Ensure the highest levels of accuracy and compliance with industry standards, coding guidelines, and regulatory requirements.
Prioritize user experience by co-designing and co-developing with end users and SMEs
Ensure data privacy and security to protect PHI data and comply with HIPAA regulations
Design scalable and flexible solutions, allowing for easy integration with existing systems / workflows
Encourage end-user feedback and monitor model performance to enhance effectiveness.
